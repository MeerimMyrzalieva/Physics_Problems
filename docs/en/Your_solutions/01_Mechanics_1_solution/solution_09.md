# Problem 9: Momentum Comparison

We compare the momenta of two objects:

* A fly of mass (2,\text{g}) moving at (10,\text{m/s})
* A tennis ball of mass (60,\text{g}) moving at (1,\text{m/s})

---

## 1) Momentum Formula

Linear momentum is defined as:

$$
p = mv
$$

where:

* (m) is mass
* (v) is velocity

---

## 2) Convert Masses to SI Units

Mass must be in kilograms.

Fly:

$$
2,\text{g} = 0.002,\text{kg}
$$

Tennis ball:

$$
60,\text{g} = 0.06,\text{kg}
$$

---

## 3) Momentum of the Fly

$$
p_{\text{fly}} = m v
= 0.002 \cdot 10
$$

$$
p_{\text{fly}} = 0.02,\text{kg·m/s}
$$

---

## 4) Momentum of the Tennis Ball

$$
p_{\text{ball}} = 0.06 \cdot 1
$$

$$
p_{\text{ball}} = 0.06,\text{kg·m/s}
$$

---

## 5) Comparison

$$
p_{\text{ball}} > p_{\text{fly}}
$$

The tennis ball has three times greater momentum.

---

## Final Answer

The object with greater momentum is:

$$
\boxed{\text{The tennis ball}}
$$
