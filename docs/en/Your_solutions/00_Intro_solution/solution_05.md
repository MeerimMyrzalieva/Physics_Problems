# Task 05 – Trigonometry of Vectors

## Problem Statement

A vector $\vec{A}$ has magnitude $|\vec{A}| = 15$ and angle $\theta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal. Find its horizontal and vertical components.

## Theory

- **Vector decomposition**:  
For a vector $\vec{A}$ in 2D:

$$
A_x = |\vec{A}| \cos \theta
$$

$$
A_y = |\vec{A}| \sin \theta
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Horizontal component**

$$
A_x = 15 \cos 60^\circ = 15 \cdot 0.5 = 7.5
$$

**Step 2: Vertical component**

$$
A_y = 15 \sin 60^\circ = 15 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 7.5 \sqrt{3}
$$

## Final Result

$$
A_x = 7.5, \quad A_y = 7.5 \sqrt{3}
$$

## Interpretation

- The horizontal and vertical components allow reconstruction of the vector in Cartesian coordinates.  
- This decomposition is fundamental in physics problems involving forces and motion.
