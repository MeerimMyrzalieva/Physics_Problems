# Task 02 – Systems of Equations

## Problem Statement

Find the values of $x$ and $y$ that satisfy the equations:

$$
2x + 3y = 12
$$

$$
x - y = 1
$$

## Theory

- **System of linear equations** can be solved using substitution or elimination.  
- **Substitution method**: Solve one equation for one variable and substitute into the other.  
- **Elimination method**: Multiply and subtract/add equations to eliminate one variable.  

## Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Solve the second equation for $x$**

$$
x = y + 1
$$

**Step 2: Substitute into the first equation**

$$
2(y + 1) + 3y = 12
$$

$$
2y + 2 + 3y = 12
$$

$$
5y + 2 = 12
$$

$$
5y = 10
$$

$$
y = 2
$$

**Step 3: Solve for $x$**

$$
x = y + 1 = 2 + 1 = 3
$$

## Final Result

$$
x = 3, \quad y = 2
$$

## Interpretation

- The solution represents the unique point $(x, y)$ where the two lines intersect.  
- Graphically, these lines would meet at $(3, 2)$ in the $xy$-plane.
