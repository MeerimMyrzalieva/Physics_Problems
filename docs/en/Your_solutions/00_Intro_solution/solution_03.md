# Task 03 — Proportionality in Gravitation

---

## Problem

Determine how the gravitational force changes if:
- distance r is doubled  
- masses m1 and m2 are halved  

---

## Step 1: Key idea

Gravitational force:
F ∝ (m1 × m2) / r²  

---

## Step 2: Change in masses

m1 → m1 / 2  
m2 → m2 / 2  

Product becomes:

m1 × m2 → (m1 × m2) / 4  

👉 Force becomes 1/4 of original  

---

## Step 3: Change in distance

r → 2r  

Then:

r² → 4r²  

👉 Force becomes 1/4 again  

---

## Step 4: Combine effects

Total change:

F_new = (1/4) × (1/4) × F  

F_new = F / 16  

---

## Final Answer

F_new = F / 16  

---

## Interpretation

- Halving both masses reduces force by 1/4  
- Doubling distance reduces force by 1/4  
- Total effect: force becomes 1/16  

👉 Distance has stronger impact due to square
