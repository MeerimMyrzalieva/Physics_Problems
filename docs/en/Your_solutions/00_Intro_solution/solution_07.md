# Task 07 – Logic & Series (Fly and Bicycle Problem)

## Problem Statement

A bicycle is 10 meters from a wall and moves towards it at $1\text{ m/s}$.  
A fly moves at $2\text{ m/s}$ back and forth between the bicycle and the wall until the bicycle reaches the wall.  

Determine the total distance the fly travels.

## Theory

- **Relative motion**: The total time until collision of bicycle with wall is $t = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$.  
- **Fly distance**: $d_{\text{fly}} = v_{\text{fly}} \cdot t_{\text{total}}$  

## Step-by-Step Solution

**Step 1: Compute total time**

$$
t_{\text{total}} = \frac{10 \text{ m}}{1 \text{ m/s}} = 10 \text{ s}
$$

**Step 2: Compute distance traveled by fly**

$$
d_{\text{fly}} = v_{\text{fly}} \cdot t_{\text{total}} = 2 \cdot 10 = 20 \text{ m}
$$

## Final Result

$$
d_{\text{fly}} = 20 \text{ m}
$$

## Interpretation

- Despite infinite back-and-forth trips, the total distance is finite.  
- This demonstrates a classical Zeno-type series in motion problems.
