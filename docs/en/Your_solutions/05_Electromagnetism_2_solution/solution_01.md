# Section 4: Electromagnetism I  
## Exercise 1 — Gauss’s Law

---

### Problem
A point charge of +2 C is located at the origin.  
Find the electric flux through a spherical surface of radius 1 m.

---

### Step 1: Formula

Φ = Q / ε₀  

---

### Step 2: Given

Q = 2 C  
ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹²  

---

### Step 3: Substitute

Φ = 2 / (8.85 × 10⁻¹²)  

---

### Step 4: Calculate

Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹  

---

### Final Answer

Φ ≈ 2.26 × 10¹¹ N·m²/C
