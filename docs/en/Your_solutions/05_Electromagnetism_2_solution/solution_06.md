# Section 4: Electromagnetism I  
## 6. EM Wave Analysis

---

## 🔹 Visual Representation



👉 This diagram shows:
- Electric field (E) along y-axis  
- Magnetic field (B) along z-axis  
- Wave propagates along x-axis  

All three are perpendicular.

---

## 🔹 Given

E_y(x,t) = 100 sin(10^7 x − ωt)

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Compare with standard form

Standard wave equation:

E(x,t) = E₀ sin(kx − ωt)

From comparison:

- E₀ = 100 V/m  
- k = 10^7 rad/m  

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Direction of propagation

Rule:

kx − ωt → wave moves in +x direction  

👉 Direction = +x  

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Wavelength

Formula:

k = 2π / λ  

λ = 2π / k  

λ = 2π / 10^7 ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m  

---

## 🔹 Step 4: Angular frequency

Formula:

ω = c k  

c = 3 × 10^8 m/s  

ω = 3 × 10^8 × 10^7 = 3 × 10^15 rad/s  

---

## 🔹 Step 5: Magnetic field

Relation:

B = E / c  

B₀ = 100 / (3 × 10^8) ≈ 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ T  

Magnetic field equation:

B_z(x,t) = 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ sin(10^7 x − 3 × 10^15 t)

---

## 🔹 Physical interpretation

- Wave propagates along x-axis  
- Electric field oscillates along y-axis  
- Magnetic field oscillates along z-axis  

👉 E ⟂ B ⟂ propagation direction  

---

## 🔹 Final Answers

- Direction: +x  
- Wavelength: λ ≈ 6.28 × 10⁻⁷ m  
- Angular frequency: ω = 3 × 10^15 rad/s  
- Magnetic field:  
  B_z(x,t) = 3.3 × 10⁻⁷ sin(10^7 x − 3 × 10^15 t)
